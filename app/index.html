My plan

The same simple day, every day.

This week's main protein

Fish 3 days for omega-3, chicken 3 days, beans or soya 1 day.

Daily template

Breakfast · 7–8am

  • 60 g oats or tom brown, with water or low-fat milk
  • 1 whole egg + 3 egg whites
  • 1 banana or orange

Lunch · around 1pm

  • 200 g skinless roasted chicken or grilled fish
  • 1 cup beans, red red (little oil) or black-eyed peas
  • Half the plate vegetables

Snack · around 4pm

  • 1 piece of fruit + a small handful of groundnuts

Dinner · around 7pm

  • 200 g grilled tilapia, mackerel, sardines or skinless chicken
  • Fist-sized banku, kenkey, yam, plantain or rice (half a fist on rest days)
  • Vegetables, pepper sauce or a light stew with little oil

Gym vs rest days

Gym day: eat your dinner carbs, or a banana with your snack, 1–2 hours before training.

Rest day: same meals, but half a fist of carbs at dinner.

Rules for life

  • Nothing fried. Very little palm oil or coconut oil.
  • No sugary drinks or juice. Water, black coffee or tea.
  • Beans or oats every single day, for your cholesterol.
  • Follow the plan about 90% of the time. After a party, go back to it at the next meal.
  • Weigh in once a week. If weight doesn't move for 3 weeks, shrink carb portions slightly.
  • Lift 3–4 times a week and slowly add weight to the bar.
  • Keep taking what your doctor prescribes. Food and medication work together.
